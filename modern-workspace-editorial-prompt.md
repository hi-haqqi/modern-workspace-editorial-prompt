# Modern Workspace — Editorial Interior Prompt

## Prompt

Create a realistic contemporary editorial interior photograph of a modern workspace, inspired by premium furniture and lifestyle product photography. The scene should feel warm, playful, sophisticated, minimal, and naturally lived-in rather than sterile or overly staged.

### Camera & Composition

- Eye-level camera positioned slightly wide, approximately 24–28mm full-frame equivalent.
- Show enough of the surrounding room to establish the environment while keeping the desk and chair as the primary subjects.
- Use natural perspective with realistic proportions and subtle depth.
- Avoid extreme wide-angle distortion.
- Compose the furniture with an intentional editorial asymmetry.
- The chair should not be perfectly aligned with the laptop; position it slightly offset or angled relative to the desk and laptop to create a more natural, candid composition.

### Furniture

Use a believable contemporary desk with clearly engineered construction.

The desk must have:

- Realistic structural support with visible legs or a substantial base.
- Correct joinery.
- Believable material thickness.
- Proper contact with the floor.
- No floating or physically impossible construction.
- Never make the desk appear to rely on only two unexplained legs.

Each variation should use a different desk design, such as:

- Rectangular timber desk
- Rounded-edge desk
- Trestle-leg desk
- Pedestal desk
- Minimalist four-leg desk

Furniture should feel functional, physically plausible, and engineered for real-world use.

### Laptop

Place a modern premium laptop naturally on the desktop, preferably slightly off-center.

Requirements:

- Screen completely blank, dark, and clean so it can function as a mockup.
- Realistic proportions.
- Subtle screen reflections.
- Accurate keyboard details.
- Natural contact shadows.
- The laptop should feel like a real physical object rather than a CGI prop.

### Styling

Add a restrained selection of everyday desk objects:

- Stacked books
- Notebooks
- Pencil holders
- Ceramic mug
- Small lamp
- Papers
- Subtle stationery
- Occasionally a glass

Arrange objects naturally with generous negative space. Avoid excessive decoration or clutter.

### Color Palette

Use a warm neutral foundation with controlled pops of:

- Grass green
- Cobalt blue
- Mustard yellow
- Terracotta
- Muted red
- Warm brown
- Cream
- Off-white

Colors should feel slightly muted and sophisticated rather than highly saturated.

Use color blocking through furniture, books, accessories, or small architectural elements.

### Materials

Use realistic contemporary materials such as:

- Natural oak or light timber
- Matte painted surfaces
- Textured fabric upholstery
- Ceramic
- Brushed metal
- Frosted or transparent glass

Materials should show subtle realistic texture and imperfections. Avoid excessive gloss.

### Lighting

Use strong but soft natural daylight entering from a large window or side opening.

Create:

- Beautiful directional sunlight
- Long, graphic architectural shadows across the wall, desk, floor, and furniture
- Physically consistent shadows based on the light direction
- Soft ambient fill so darker areas still retain detail

### Environment

Create a minimal contemporary interior with:

- Warm off-white or light beige walls
- Pale neutral flooring
- Occasional timber cabinetry
- Minimal shelving
- Indoor plants
- Restrained abstract artwork

Keep the background clean and uncluttered.

The architecture should feel modern but comfortable, not luxurious or corporate.

### Photography Style

High-end editorial furniture photography with:

- Contemporary lifestyle photography
- Scandinavian-inspired product styling
- Subtle analog photography character
- Realistic textures
- Natural imperfections
- Sophisticated art direction
- Soft filmic tonal range
- Crisp but not artificially sharp detail
- Premium commercial photography

### Human Presence

Prefer no visible person.

When useful, a subtle partial human presence is acceptable, but:

- Crop the person naturally.
- Keep the person secondary to the furniture.
- Do not make the person the focal point.

### Overall Aesthetic

**Warm editorial minimalism × playful contemporary design × realistic everyday workspace**

The result should feel like a sophisticated furniture brand campaign photographed in a real creative studio or modern home office.

## Important Constraints

Prioritize:

- Realistic furniture construction
- Realistic perspective
- Realistic object scale
- Realistic contact shadows
- Physically plausible architecture
- Clean composition
- Natural material behavior
- No floating objects
- No impossible furniture geometry
- No excessive clutter
- No sterile corporate office aesthetic
- No exaggerated CGI look
- No overly dramatic luxury styling
