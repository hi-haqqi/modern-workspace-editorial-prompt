## Overview

This repository contains a structured image-generation prompt for realistic contemporary workspace photography.

The visual direction combines:

- Warm editorial minimalism
- Playful contemporary furniture
- Scandinavian-inspired styling
- Premium commercial photography
- Natural daylight and architectural shadows
- Realistic furniture construction and object scale
- Subtle analog / filmic character

The goal is to create images that feel **photographed in a real creative studio or modern home office**, rather than rendered as polished CGI.

## Core Visual Direction

**Warm editorial minimalism × playful contemporary design × realistic everyday workspace**

## Recommended Usage

Use the prompt as a base and modify only the sections needed for a specific generation.

### Keep consistent

- Realistic perspective
- Physically plausible furniture
- Natural contact shadows
- Warm neutral foundation
- Controlled color accents
- Soft directional daylight
- Editorial asymmetry
- Clean negative space

### Vary intentionally

- Desk construction
- Chair position
- Camera framing
- Architectural details
- Accessories
- Color-blocking placement
- Amount of visible environment

## Variation Strategy

Change one or two variables at a time to maintain a cohesive visual series.

| Variation | Desk | Composition | Accent |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Rectangular timber | Wide editorial | Grass green |
| 02 | Rounded-edge | Slightly tighter | Cobalt blue |
| 03 | Trestle-leg | Asymmetrical | Mustard yellow |
| 04 | Pedestal | Environmental | Terracotta |
| 05 | Four-leg | Desk-focused | Muted red |

## Quality Checklist

- [ ] Desk construction is physically believable
- [ ] Furniture has proper floor contact
- [ ] Laptop proportions feel realistic
- [ ] Objects have believable scale
- [ ] Shadows follow a consistent light direction
- [ ] Perspective does not feel distorted
- [ ] Materials have subtle texture
- [ ] Background remains clean
- [ ] Color accents remain controlled
- [ ] Image does not look excessively CGI
- [ ] Styling does not become cluttered
- [ ] Furniture remains the visual focus

## Intended Use

Suitable for furniture campaigns, interior concepts, workspace moodboards, editorial art direction, product mockups, website hero imagery, brand campaigns, creative studio environments, and AI image-generation workflows.
